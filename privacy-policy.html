The short version

RizzDaily has no backend server of its own for your content: your onboarding answers, journal notes, mission history, streaks, and settings are written to a private database and preference store on your device, and we never receive them. Two things do move data off the device:

  • Analytics (PostHog) — basic product-usage events (which screens/features you open), never the content you write. Off by default if you're in Europe (you're asked first); on by default elsewhere, with your own choice always available and always overriding that default. Fully explained under "Analytics" below.
  • Android's Auto Backup, an OS service rather than anything RizzDaily itself runs, copies your on-device storage (except your analytics identifier and analytics choice) to your own Google Drive. Explained further down.

What's stored on your device

All of this lives in a local Room database (wingman.db) and local DataStore preferences, inside the app's private storage, accessible only to RizzDaily:

  • Onboarding answers: age range, how you heard about the app, goals, challenges, confidence level, social frequency, and similar free-text/multiple-choice answers; a commitment date; and, if you draw one, a "commitment signature" stored as the raw stroke points (not an image), or a typed name instead.
  • Notes and reflections: anything you write in the journal, including mission reflections ("what went well" / "what to improve") and free-text daily focus goals.
  • Mission history and progress: assigned/accepted/passed/completed missions, roadmap progress, streak, unlocked achievements, and any custom mission you write yourself (title and description).
  • App settings: theme, language, reminder time, sounds/reminders on or off.

Android backup (Google Drive)

RizzDaily currently allows Android's Auto Backup, so the private storage described above — your onboarding answers, notes, mission history, and settings — can be copied to your own Google Drive and restored automatically on a new device. We chose to leave this on so you keep your progress if you switch phones. A few things worth knowing:

  • The backup is encrypted by Android and stored under your Google account, governed by Google's own backup policy — RizzDaily's developers cannot access it.
  • Your analytics identifier and your analytics choice (see "Analytics" below) are excluded from this backup, so they never travel to a new device.
  • You can turn this off at any time in Android Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete an existing Drive backup. Android removes old app backups from Drive on its own schedule, or you can delete RizzDaily's backup yourself from that same Backup screen in Drive.

Permissions we ask for, and why

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — your reminders: the daily mission reminder, the optional morning reminder, and a one-time reminder the day before a free trial ends (sent only if your subscription is still set to renew). The daily and morning reminders can be turned off in Profile settings, and all notifications in Android's own settings.
  • Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — re-schedules your reminders after a restart; does not otherwise run the app in the background.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — used to send analytics events (see "Analytics" below) when analytics is currently on and, if you signed in with Google, to download your Google profile photo from Google's servers so Profile can show it. Signing in with Google and buying a subscription are handled by Google Play services and the Google Play Store app on your device. Nothing else in the app makes a network call.

RizzDaily's own code requests no other permissions. A few more end up in the installed app anyway, declared by libraries rather than by us: com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play Billing, used for subscriptions), ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (the PostHog analytics SDK, to check connectivity before sending), WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICE (AndroidX WorkManager, which schedules the reminders above), and USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT (Android's Credential Manager library behind "Continue with Google"; RizzDaily itself never asks for, or has access to, fingerprint or face data). None of them let the app collect anything beyond what's already described here.

Accounts

RizzDaily has no account server of its own. Onboarding's "Continue with Google" uses Android's own Credential Manager to sign in with an existing Google account on your device — RizzDaily never sees your Google password, and Google's own account picker handles the sign-in itself. "Continue without an account" skips this and creates the same local, on-device profile with no name or photo attached.

What we keep, and where. Your Google display name and profile photo URL (if your account has one) are stored only on this device, in a small local preferences file — never on any server we run — so Profile can show your real name and photo instead of the generic "You". Nothing about your Google account (no email, no password, no contacts) is ever read beyond the name and photo Google itself hands back. See "Analytics" below for the one other thing a Google sign-in contributes: a one-way hashed version of your Google account's internal id, used only as an anonymous-looking analytics key when analytics is on.

Removing it. Profile → Log Out or Delete Account both clear Android's Credential Manager sign-in state for RizzDaily and erase the locally stored name/photo, so your next sign-in starts fresh rather than silently reusing the old one.

Analytics

RizzDaily uses PostHog (posthog.com) for basic product analytics — understanding which screens and features people use, so we know what to build next. No advertising and no crash-reporting SDK are included, and no third-party SDK reads your stored on-device data (onboarding answers, notes, mission history) — analytics only ever sees the events described below.

Region-based default, with your own choice always available. If you're in the EU, the rest of the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, analytics is off by default: RizzDaily asks during onboarding, and nothing is sent — and no identifier is created — until you say yes. Everywhere else, analytics is on by default, the way most apps work, and you can turn it off at any time. Either way, your own explicit answer (from the onboarding question, or the Profile "Analytics" toggle) always overrides the regional default, in both directions.

What's sent, while it's on — every event RizzDaily currently records, and nothing else:

  • Which screens/features you open (e.g. "viewed History") — never the content of anything you write; onboarding answers, notes, and mission text always stay on-device, as described above.
  • Signing up (whether you continued with the Google option or without an account) and finishing onboarding (your chosen skill level, and your "when do you want results" timeline answer) — never any of onboarding's other, more personal answers.
  • Accepting, passing, or completing a mission (its category, your skill level, and whether it was a mission you wrote yourself — never the mission's own instruction text) and creating a custom mission (its category and how many reps you set it to — never the text you typed).
  • Saving a journal note (only whether it was tied to a mission or written standalone — never the note's title or either reflection answer) and unlocking an achievement (which badge, by its internal key, e.g. "first_step" — never your notes about it).
  • Changing your skill level (the level you moved from and to) and opening a day on the 30-day roadmap (which day number — never its content).
  • An identifier for this install — never your email, name, or photo. If you signed in with "Continue without an account", this is a random id tied to the install, not to you. If you signed in with Google, this is instead a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Google account's internal id — irreversible, and never the account id itself, your email, or your name — so the same Google account produces the same analytics id if you reinstall and sign in again, without RizzDaily or PostHog ever seeing the real account id. Either way, deleting your data or turning analytics off deletes RizzDaily's own copy of this identifier.
  • Basic device/app info PostHog's SDK attaches to every event automatically (device model, OS version, app version, screen size, locale, and similar technical details) — no advertising ID and no precise (GPS/Wi-Fi) location. Every event's network request does carry your IP address to PostHog, though, and PostHog's server-side GeoIP lookup uses it to attach an approximate location (country/region/city) to the event — the same kind of estimate most web/app analytics and even ordinary web server logs produce from any visitor's IP, not something RizzDaily requests a location permission for or reads from the device itself.

Subscriptions. RizzDaily is a paid, subscription-based app (Google Play Billing). Whether your subscription is currently active is stored on-device, the same as everything else described above — there is no backend, so purchases are never verified anywhere except directly with Google Play. When analytics is on, purchase-related events are sent the same way as everything else above: paywall_viewed (where the paywall was shown from), trial_started/purchase_completed (which plan, and — for a completed purchase — the price and currency), and subscription_restored. None of this includes your name, email, or payment details — RizzDaily never sees your payment information at all: entering a card, PayPal, or other payment method happens entirely inside Google Play, governed by Google's own privacy policy, and Play only ever tells the app whether a purchase succeeded, not how you paid for it.

Processor and location. Events are sent to PostHog's US cloud (us.i.posthog.com). PostHog is our data processor for this purpose only, bound by its own privacy policy, and does not use your data for its own separate purposes.

Turning it off. Decline the onboarding question, or switch Profile's "Analytics" toggle off — either way, RizzDaily tells PostHog to stop and immediately deletes our own on-device identifier (the one described above). Turning it back on later starts fresh, with a brand-new identifier.

Retention and deletion. We control what's described above: turning analytics off, or deleting your account in Profile, stops future events and deletes our own local identifier right away. Two things that doesn't cover: it does not retroactively delete events already sent to PostHog — that's governed by PostHog's own retention policy; contact us and we'll pass along a deletion request on your behalf — and it does not delete an internal device id the PostHog SDK itself keeps (separate from our own identifier above); that id is disconnected from you either way, since it's no longer paired with anything we send once analytics is off.

RizzDaily also uses Google Play's In-App Review feature to occasionally ask for a rating: that rating dialog is drawn and operated entirely by the Google Play Store app, not by RizzDaily — nothing from RizzDaily's stored data is passed to it, and whatever star rating or review text you choose to submit there goes directly to Google, under Google's own privacy policy. If that dialog can't be shown (for example, when the app wasn't installed from Google Play), RizzDaily opens its own Google Play Store page instead, so you can rate it there.

How long we keep data, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device (and, unless you've turned off Auto Backup, in your own Google Drive) for as long as the app and that backup exist. To delete it:

  • In-app: Profile → Delete Account permanently erases your missions, notes, roadmap progress, achievements, and settings on the device, and erases your analytics identifier and choice too (see "Analytics" above). "Log Out" is lighter — it signs you out (clearing the Google sign-in and the stored name/photo), starts a new analytics session, and resets onboarding, but keeps your missions, notes, and progress on the device if you come back.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its private storage on the device, per standard Android behavior — but see "Android backup" above: it does not by itself delete a Google Drive backup, which you can remove from Android Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Analytics: see "Analytics" above — turning it off, in onboarding or from the Profile toggle, deletes your on-device analytics identifier right away; it does not retroactively delete events already sent to PostHog.

Children's privacy

Onboarding's age question starts at 18–24 (then 25–34, 35–44, Over 45); RizzDaily is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, anyone under 18.
